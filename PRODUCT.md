# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences reach the site, usually from a LinkedIn link, a resume or a client referral:

- **Recruiters and HR**: non-technical, skimming many profiles. They decide in seconds whether to keep reading, so role, stack and seniority must be legible at a glance.
- **Tech leads and developers**: technical reviewers who open live projects and GitHub repos and judge depth. They reward specifics (architecture, tests, security decisions) and punish fluff.
- **Prospective TLG-Web-Dev clients**: small-business owners judging whether this person can build *their* site. The portfolio itself is the proof of craft.

Target roles: Backend Python (FastAPI/Flask) and full-stack web/mobile (React, TypeScript, Capacitor). Embedded/IoT work is real background, not the headline.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Leonardo Gotardo, a software developer in Londrina, Brazil (EN-first, PT available). It exists to turn a visit into a conversation: the primary success action is **getting in touch** (email or LinkedIn). Downloading the resume and opening live projects are supporting actions on the way there.

## Brand Personality

Creative and bold. The site should be memorable and have a strong point of view, while staying credible to engineers: confident, specific, a little playful, never noisy.

## Anti-references

- **Generic portfolio template**: hero + about + skill pills + identical project cards, the layout every dev clones.
- **AI-generated look**: default dark-navy + cyan/orange gradients, glow on everything, card grids, filler copy.
- **Gamer / neon overload**: particle backgrounds, mouse glows, heavy text-shadows and effects competing for attention.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't list.** Evidence beats inventories: a project's real decisions and results say more than 136 skill pills.
2. **Bold through structure, not effects.** Personality comes from layout, typography and color commitment, not from glows and particles.
3. **Respect the skim.** A recruiter must get name, role, target stack and a way to contact within one screen; depth is there for whoever scrolls.
4. **Conversation is the goal.** Every section should make reaching out feel like the obvious next step.
5. **Honest specifics.** Numbers and claims must be verifiable. IrisPay work is under NDA and stays at the technology level.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), full keyboard navigation with visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for all decorative animation (particles, glows, reveals), both dark and light themes held to the same contrast bar.
